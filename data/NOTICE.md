# AI4I 2020 dataset attribution

The bundled `ai4i2020.csv` is the unmodified CSV from the UCI Machine Learning
Repository's AI4I 2020 Predictive Maintenance Dataset (dataset 601).

Creator: Stephan Matzka. Year: 2020.

Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/601/ai4i+2020+predictive+maintenance+dataset

DOI: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5HS5C

License of the UCI distribution: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The dataset is synthetic. MaintAI creates stratified partitions and engineered
features at runtime, but does not modify the bundled original CSV. Its replay
is a simulation, not live factory data. Dataset attribution does not imply
endorsement of MaintAI by the creator or UCI.
